/*
    Operation: ROLL UP
    Question: What was total revenue for all of 2021?
    Business meaning: The direct mirror of Drill Down #1 -- instead
    of breaking the year into its months, this collapses all six
    active months back up into a single yearly figure. Since SSAS
    pre-aggregates every level of a hierarchy automatically,
    referencing the Year member itself (rather than its Children)
    returns the sum with no extra calculation needed.

    Verification: this result (1,745,677) exactly matches both the
    sum of all six months from Drill Down #1, and the very first
    whole-cube total query used to confirm the cube connection at
    the start of Phase 5 -- three independent checks agreeing.
*/

SELECT
    [Measures].[Price] ON COLUMNS,
    [Departure Date].[Calendar].[Year].&[2021] ON ROWS
FROM [FlightAirticketCube]
