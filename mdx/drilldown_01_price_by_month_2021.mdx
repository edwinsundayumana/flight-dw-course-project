/*
    Operation: DRILL DOWN
    Question: What was total revenue for 2021, broken down into
    its individual months?
    Business meaning: The most direct demonstration of drill down --
    starting from one summary figure (the year) and moving to a
    more detailed level (its months) within the same hierarchy.

    Uses the Calendar hierarchy (Year -> Month -> Date) built in the
    cube designer specifically for this kind of navigation, and the
    .Children function to request exactly the level one step below
    a given member -- the idiomatic MDX way to express "drill down
    into this member," rather than requesting a whole level and
    separately trying to filter it (which caused a "hierarchy
    already appears in axis" error on the first attempt).
*/

SELECT
    [Measures].[Price] ON COLUMNS,
    [Departure Date].[Calendar].[Year].&[2021].Children ON ROWS
FROM [FlightAirticketCube]
