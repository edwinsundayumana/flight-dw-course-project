/*
    Operation: TOP N
    Question: Who are the top 5 airlines by total revenue?
    Business meaning: More directly useful than a full ranking for
    most real reporting -- stakeholders usually want "show me the
    winners," not a ranked list of every single airline.

    TopCount(set, N, measure) sorts a set by the given measure and
    returns only the top N in one step, simpler than building a
    Rank() calculated member when you only need the top few.
    .[All].Children (not .MEMBERS) keeps the "All" aggregate out of
    contention.

    Verified: matches the top 5 entries of Rank #1's full ranking
    exactly (四川航空, 中国南方航空, 吉祥航空, 春秋航空, 海南航空).
*/

SELECT
    [Measures].[Price] ON COLUMNS,
    TopCount( [Dim Airlines].[Airlines].[All].Children, 5, [Measures].[Price] ) ON ROWS
FROM [FlightAirticketCube]
