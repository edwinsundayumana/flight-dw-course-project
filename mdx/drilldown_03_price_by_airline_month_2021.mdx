/*
    Operation: DRILL DOWN
    Question: What was total revenue by airline for 2021, drilled
    down to show each airline's monthly breakdown?
    Business meaning: A richer, report-style drill down -- combines
    the Year->Month hierarchy drill with a second dimension
    (Airline) at the same time, showing not just "the months" but
    "each airline's performance within each month."

    Introduces the cross-join operator (*), which pairs every
    member of one set with every member of another (every airline x
    every month), and NON EMPTY, which suppresses the many
    airline/month combinations that have no actual data -- without
    it, the result would include a large number of blank rows.
*/

SELECT
    [Measures].[Price] ON COLUMNS,
    NON EMPTY
    [Dim Airlines].[Airlines].MEMBERS *
    [Departure Date].[Calendar].[Year].&[2021].Children
    ON ROWS
FROM [FlightAirticketCube]
