/*
    Operation: DRILL DOWN
    Question: What was the flight count for March 2021, drilled
    down into individual days?
    Business meaning: Goes one level deeper than example 1 (Month
    -> Date), showing exactly which day(s) within a month actually
    had activity -- useful for spotting concentrated demand (e.g.
    a single event or holiday driving a whole month's flights).

    Chains two .Children-style navigations: first to the specific
    Month member (key 3 = March) under Year 2021, then to its
    Children (the individual dates).
*/

SELECT
    [Measures].[Fact Airticket Count] ON COLUMNS,
    [Departure Date].[Calendar].[Year].&[2021].[3].Children ON ROWS
FROM [FlightAirticketCube]
